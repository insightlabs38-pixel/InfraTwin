## Editing principles

- Product UI remains the visual centerpiece.
- Hard cuts and staged reveals dominate; no wipes, glitch effects, fake terminals, stock footage, or fabricated ChatGPT UI.
- The lock → stale → replan sequence is kept intact at real recorded timing.
- Performance and scale claims use only numbers supplied by the frozen evidence base.
- Product footage numbers are never altered or composited.
